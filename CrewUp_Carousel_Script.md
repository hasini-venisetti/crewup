# CrewUp – Social Media Carousel Script
### "How to Use CrewUp" — Feature Walkthrough Carousel
*For Instagram / LinkedIn / Twitter*

---

## 🎠 CAROUSEL OPTION 1 — "Step by Step" Style
*(Clean, minimal, educational)*

---

**SLIDE 1 — Hook / Cover**
> # 🎬 Everything you need to know about CrewUp
> ### The platform built for film creators
> *Swipe to explore every feature →*

---

**SLIDE 2 — Sign Up**
> ## Step 1: Create Your Account
> Pick your role — Director, Actor, Writer, Editor and more
> Add your name, username & email
> Verify your phone & email to get **verified badges** on your profile ✅

---

**SLIDE 3 — Your Profile**
> ## Step 2: Build Your Identity
> Upload your photo
> Write a bio that shows your vision
> Add **multiple roles** — Director + Writer + Actor? Show them all
> Set your availability so people know when to reach you

---

**SLIDE 4 — Explore**
> ## Step 3: Discover Creators
> Browse filmmakers, actors, editors & more
> Filter by **role, skill, location & availability**
> Follow creators you vibe with
> Every refresh = a new set of creators 🔀

---

**SLIDE 5 — Projects**
> ## Step 4: Find Your Next Project
> Browse open film projects looking for crew
> Filter by **genre, role, location & pay**
> See open roles + descriptions before you apply
> Or **post your own project** and build your team

---

**SLIDE 6 — Films**
> ## Step 5: Share Your Work
> Upload your short films, reels & trailers
> Like, comment & bookmark other creators' work
> Your films appear on your **public portfolio**
> Get seen. Get noticed. Get hired.

---

**SLIDE 7 — Mentors**
> ## Step 6: Learn from the Best
> Find mentors in Directing, Cinematography, Writing & more
> Book **1-on-1 sessions** — online or in-person
> See their rating, experience & session types upfront
> Or **become a mentor** and share your expertise

---

**SLIDE 8 — Messages**
> ## Step 7: Connect Directly
> Send **message requests** to any creator
> Once accepted — real-time chat, no noise
> Coordinate collabs, discuss projects, close deals

---

**SLIDE 9 — Contests**
> ## Bonus: Join Contests 🏆
> CrewUp runs creative challenges for the community
> Compete, get your work seen & win recognition
> Check the Contests tab regularly for new drops

---

**SLIDE 10 — CTA**
> # Ready to build your film career?
> ## Join CrewUp today
> 🔗 crewupfilms.com
> *Find your crew. Tell your story.*
