How to Submit Your
Short Film

Complete step-by-step guide — from login to submission

01
Log In to CrewUp
Sign in at crewup.in. New here? Create a free account first — it only takes a minute.
02
Build Your Team
Post a project and accept applicants. You need at least 2 team members (yourself + 1 accepted member) to be eligible.
03
Upload Film to YouTube
Set your video to Public or Unlisted. Your YouTube description must include:
📋
① The word "crewupfilms" (or "crewup films")
② Your CrewUp @username
Missing either will auto-reject your submission.
04
Go to Contests
Click Contests in the top navigation bar and open the Short Film Contest.
05
Click "Submit Your Film"
Hit the Submit Your Film button. The system auto-checks your eligibility in seconds.
06
Fill the Submission Form
Enter your Film Title, paste your YouTube link, and add an optional message for the community.
07
Submit & Await Review
Click 🎬 Submit Film. You'll get a notification once approved — or guidance if changes are needed.
08
Share & Collect Votes! 🎉
Once approved, share your film card with friends and community. Public votes count toward your final score!
