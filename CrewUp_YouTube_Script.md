# CrewUp – Full Platform Walkthrough Script
### YouTube Video Script | "How to Use CrewUp – Complete Guide"
---

> **Suggested runtime:** 18–25 minutes
> **Format:** Screen recording + voiceover
> **Tone:** Friendly, enthusiastic, clear — like a friend showing you around
> 📌 *[ON SCREEN]* = what to show on screen
> 🎙️ = voiceover / what you say

---

## ▶ INTRO (0:00 – 0:45)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: CrewUp homepage / landing page, logo animation]*

🎙️
"Hey everyone, welcome back! Today I'm going to walk you through **CrewUp** — the platform built specifically for filmmakers, creators, writers, actors, editors, and every kind of creative professional in the film space.

Whether you're looking to collaborate on a project, find a mentor, showcase your work, or just connect with other creators — CrewUp has everything in one place.

So let's start from the very beginning — signing up — and I'll take you through every single feature, step by step.

Let's get into it."

---

## 📝 SECTION 1 — SIGN UP (0:45 – 2:30)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Click "Sign Up" button on homepage]*

🎙️
"First things first — let's create your account. Hit the **Sign Up** button right here."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Sign up form — name, username, email, password, role, gender fields]*

🎙️
"You'll fill in your:
- Full name
- A unique **username** — this is your public handle on the platform, so pick something you like
- Your **email address**
- A **password**
- Your **primary role** — so are you a Director? Cinematographer? Actor? Writer? Pick what best describes you
- And your **gender** — this is used for things like crew matching and casting, so the right roles show up for you

Once you've filled everything in, hit **Create Account**."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Success message / redirect to dashboard or email verification prompt]*

🎙️
"And just like that, you're in! Now there are two verification steps I want to show you — **email** and **phone** — and I'd strongly recommend doing both, because they unlock features on the platform."

---

## ✉️ SECTION 2 — EMAIL VERIFICATION (2:30 – 3:30)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Settings or verification prompt — "Verify your email"]*

🎙️
"Email verification is straightforward. You'll get a link sent to your inbox — just click it and you're verified.

Once verified, you'll see a small **Email badge** appear on your profile — a blue checkmark that tells other users your account is legit."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Profile page showing the blue "✓ Email" badge]*

🎙️
"See that? That blue badge — **'Email Verified'**. It builds trust, especially when you're collaborating with people you've never met before."

---

## 📱 SECTION 3 — PHONE VERIFICATION (3:30 – 4:30)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Phone verification section — enter phone number, get OTP]*

🎙️
"Phone verification works through an **OTP — a one-time password** sent to your mobile number.

Enter your 10-digit Indian mobile number here — no need to add the country code, the system handles that automatically.

You'll get a text message with a 6-digit code. Enter it here and hit **Verify**."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Green "✓ Phone" badge on profile]*

🎙️
"Once verified, you get a green **Phone badge** on your profile. Both these badges are visible to anyone who views your profile — it's a quick signal that you're a real, verified creator."

---

## 👤 SECTION 4 — SETTING UP YOUR PROFILE (4:30 – 7:00)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Edit Profile]*

🎙️
"Now let's set up your profile properly — this is your creative identity on the platform, so let's make it count."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Profile edit form — photo, bio, roles, location, skills, level]*

🎙️
"**Profile Photo** — Upload a clear photo or headshot. First impressions matter.

**Bio** — Write a few lines about who you are, what kind of work you do, and what you're looking for. Be specific — 'Independent filmmaker passionate about character-driven stories' is way better than just 'I make films.'

**Roles** — This is important. You can select **multiple roles**. So if you're a Director who also writes scripts and sometimes acts — tick all three. They'll all show up on your profile."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Multiple roles selected — Director, Writer, Actor all showing as pills/tags]*

🎙️
"See how all three roles appear here? Other creators can immediately understand your range.

**Availability** — Set this to Available, Open to Offers, or Busy so people know whether to approach you.

**Location** — Add your city and state. This helps with local collaborations.

**Experience Level** — Are you a Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced creator? Set it honestly.

**Skills** — Add your specific skills here, like 'Final Cut Pro', 'DSLR Shooting', 'Adobe Premiere', 'Screenwriting' — whatever you're good at.

Once everything looks good, hit **Save**."

---

## 🌐 SECTION 5 — YOUR PUBLIC PROFILE (7:00 – 8:30)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to your public profile page]*

🎙️
"This is what everyone else sees when they visit your profile. Let me walk you through it.

At the top you've got your **name**, **username**, your **roles** as tags, and those **verified badges** we set up earlier.

Below that — your **follower and following counts**. Click on either to see the full list of who's following you or who you follow."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Click followers — modal opens with list of followers]*

🎙️
"Right here — a clean modal showing all your followers. Click any name to go to their profile."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Scroll down to Work & Credentials section]*

🎙️
"Scrolling down, this is the **Work & Credentials** section — where you showcase your actual work. Portfolio pieces, past projects, certifications, whatever demonstrates your craft.

Hit **Add Work** to start building your portfolio."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Share Portfolio button]*

🎙️
"There's also a **Share Portfolio** button — one click gives you a shareable link to your profile. Send it to a director, a casting team, or put it in your Instagram bio."

---

## 🔍 SECTION 6 — EXPLORE PAGE (8:30 – 10:00)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Explore tab]*

🎙️
"Now let's look at the **Explore page** — this is where you discover other creators on the platform.

Every card here is a verified creative professional. You can see their name, their roles, their location, and their availability status."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Scroll through creator cards]*

🎙️
"The **green dot** means Available, **purple** means Open to Offers, and **red** means Busy.

You can use the **search bar** to look for someone by name or username, or filter by role — looking for a cinematographer in Mumbai? Filter it down."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Click on a creator's card → go to their profile]*

🎙️
"Click any card to go to their full profile — see their work, their credentials, and if you want to collaborate, you can **follow** them or send a **message request** right from there."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Previous / Next pagination at bottom]*

🎙️
"At the bottom, you've got **pagination** — Previous and Next — so you can browse through all the creators on the platform."

---

## 🎬 SECTION 7 — PROJECTS (10:00 – 12:00)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Projects tab]*

🎙️
"The **Projects** section is the heart of collaboration on CrewUp. This is where creators post their upcoming films, short films, web series, music videos — any project that needs a crew."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Browse project cards]*

🎙️
"Each card shows you the project title, genre, shooting location, and which **roles are open** — so you know at a glance if there's a place for you.

Use the filters at the top to narrow down by genre, location, or role type."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Click on a project card → project detail page]*

🎙️
"Click a project to open the **detail page**. Here you'll see the full description, shooting dates, compensation details, and a complete breakdown of every role they're hiring for.

Each role comes with a **description** — so you know exactly what's expected before you apply."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Apply button for a role]*

🎙️
"See a role that fits you? Hit **Apply** and send your application directly to the project creator."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Post a Project — click '+ Post Project' button]*

🎙️
"And if **you're** the one creating something — hit **Post Project** up here. Fill in your project details, add the roles you're looking for, write a description for each role, and publish. Your project will be visible to every creator on the platform."

---

## 🎥 SECTION 8 — FILMS (12:00 – 13:30)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Films tab]*

🎙️
"The **Films** tab is your showcase wall — where creators upload their completed work. Short films, reels, trailers, music videos — if you made it, share it here.

You can **like**, **comment**, **bookmark**, and **share** any film."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Click like button, comment section]*

🎙️
"The community feed here is a great way to discover new talent — you might find your next cinematographer just by watching someone's short film.

And of course, **upload your own work** to let people see what you can do. Your films also appear on your public profile under Work & Credentials."

---

## 🎓 SECTION 9 — MENTORS (13:30 – 16:30)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Mentors tab]*

🎙️
"This is one of the most unique features on CrewUp — the **Mentors section**.

Here you can find experienced professionals who are open to mentoring emerging creators — for portfolio reviews, career guidance, script feedback, and more."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Browse mentor cards — showing fields, session types, rates, availability]*

🎙️
"Each mentor card shows you their **specialisation** — Directing, Cinematography, Screenwriting and so on — along with:
- Their **experience level**
- Session types they offer — like 1-on-1 video calls, portfolio reviews, or project guidance
- Whether they're **online-only, in-person, or both**
- Their **rate** per hour, or if they're offering free sessions
- Their **rating** from past mentees"

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Click on a mentor card — Book Session button]*

🎙️
"Find someone whose expertise matches what you need? Hit **Book Session**.

You'll pick a **date from their available range**, choose a **time slot**, select the session type, add any notes about what you want to discuss — and send the booking request."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: In-person session — address revealed after booking confirmed]*

🎙️
"One thing worth noting — if you book an **in-person session**, the mentor's exact address is only revealed **after they confirm your booking**. This protects everyone's privacy.

For online sessions, the meeting link is shared after confirmation."

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Mentors Dashboard — 'Become a Mentor' or 'My Dashboard']*

🎙️
"Now — if **you** want to become a mentor, scroll down and click **Become a Mentor**, or if you're already one, go to your **Mentor Dashboard**.

Here you can set:
- Your fields and specialisations
- Session types you offer
- Your availability — which months you're open for bookings
- Specific dates you want to **block off** — holidays, shoot days, whatever
- Your rate
- And your mentor status — Active, Paused, or Closed"

---

## 💬 SECTION 10 — MESSAGES (16:30 – 17:30)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Messages]*

🎙️
"The **Messages** feature lets you communicate directly with other creators — but with one important layer: **message requests**.

Before someone can message you freely, they send a request. You either accept or decline. This keeps your inbox clean and spam-free.

Once accepted, it's a standard real-time chat — great for coordinating on projects, discussing collaborations, or following up after a booking."

---

## 🔔 SECTION 11 — NOTIFICATIONS (17:30 – 18:15)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Click Notifications bell icon]*

🎙️
"The **Notifications bell** keeps you updated on everything — new followers, project applications, message requests, booking confirmations, and more.

Everything in one place, so you never miss an important update."

---

## 🏆 SECTION 12 — CONTESTS (18:15 – 19:00)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Contests section]*

🎙️
"CrewUp also runs **Contests** — creative challenges open to the community. Check this section regularly for active contests, entry requirements, prizes, and deadlines.

It's a great way to push your creativity, get your work seen, and compete with other creators on the platform."

---

## ⚙️ SECTION 13 — SETTINGS & ACCOUNT (19:00 – 19:45)

📌 *[ON SCREEN: Navigate to Settings / Account]*

🎙️
"In your **Settings**, you can update your password, manage your notification preferences, and control your account details.

If at any point you want to take a break, you can deactivate your account from here as well."
